Copyright takedown notice procedure

Our Position

SnapDrop does not host, store, upload, or distribute any video or audio content. We provide a tool that generates download links for publicly accessible media. We respect intellectual property rights and comply with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

Filing a DMCA Notice

If you believe content accessible through our service infringes your copyright, please send a notice to dmca@snapdrop.com containing:

  • Your name, address, and contact information
  • Description of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed
  • The URL of the infringing content
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized
  • A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate
  • Your electronic or physical signature

Response Time

We will respond to valid DMCA notices within 2-3 business days. We take copyright seriously and will act promptly on verified claims.
