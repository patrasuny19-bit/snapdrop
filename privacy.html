Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SnapDrop does not require registration or account creation. We do not collect personally identifiable information. When you use our service, we may collect:

  • URLs you submit for processing (not stored after processing)
  • Browser type and operating system (via standard server logs)
  • IP address (anonymized, for security and abuse prevention)
  • Usage data via Google Analytics (anonymized)

2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to:

  • Process your video download requests
  • Improve our service and user experience
  • Prevent abuse and ensure security
  • Display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out via Google Ad Settings. We also use Google Analytics to understand traffic patterns. All data is anonymized.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party APIs to process video links. These services may log request data per their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share your data with any third party for marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

We do not store downloaded video files on our servers. Video URLs submitted are not retained after processing is complete. Server logs are deleted after 30 days.

6. Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there is nothing to access, modify, or delete. If you have concerns, contact us at privacy@snapdrop.com.

7. Children's Privacy

SnapDrop is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes are posted on this page with a revised date. Continued use of SnapDrop after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
