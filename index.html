Questions
Is SnapDrop free to use?

Yes, completely. There are no hidden fees or account requirements. You can download as many videos as you need at no cost.

Is it legal to download social media videos?

Downloading is generally fine for personal, offline use. Do not redistribute, re-upload, or commercialize content you do not own. Respect copyright and the terms of each platform.

What is the maximum video quality available?

Up to the source resolution — we offer everything from 360p up to 4K (2160p) where the platform provides it. The quality options shown reflect what's actually available for that specific video.

Do you store downloaded content on your servers?

No. Files stream directly to your device. We act only as a bridge — nothing is retained on our end after your session ends.
